# Historical design and intent

These records explain the predecessor and the intent behind Omux. They are not
current setup instructions, implementation contracts, execution policy or proof
of the native reset. The [October 2 reset](../plans/omux-native-account-lifecycle-reset-2026-10-02.md)
is the active architecture; it is experimental and unshipped.

The [retired-design inventory](retired-design-inventory.md) records every legacy
document disposition in this cleanup. A small number remain at their original
paths because historical release evidence refers to them. Their old commands,
managed-launch assumptions, optional-daemon design and provider support claims
must not be applied to the replacement. Section 0 of the retained broker contract
preserves the same-process product outcome; its wrapper invocation is superseded.

Research preserves dated original intent, including TIN-491 and TIN-2057 context.
It does not confer implementation authority. Deleted operational prose remains
recoverable from the pre-reset Git baseline and release tags. Reviewed evidence,
fixtures and CHANGELOG remain in their original locations with their original
claim boundaries.
